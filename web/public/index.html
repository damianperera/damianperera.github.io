How I work

Before I write a line, I draw.

Chalk is cheap; production is not. I sketch the flow first — the boxes, the arrows, the thing that fails at 2 a.m. — until the design is boring enough to trust. Then I build it, these days with a lot of agentic AI in the loop. But the thinking still starts on the board.
